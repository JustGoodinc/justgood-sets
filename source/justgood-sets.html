Getting started

Two demo loops are loaded when the page opens, so you can press PLAY on deck 1 straight away.

Load a track
Press LOAD on a deck to open the library for that deck, then double-click a track or press its 1 or 2. OPEN AUDIO FILE in the library picks a single file instead. You can also drop a file onto a deck or its track bar. MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and FLAC work in most browsers.
Record your mix
Press ● REC at the top of the page to record exactly what the crowd hears, after the master knob. Press it again to stop, then SAVE MIX to keep the recording. On a phone that opens the share sheet, so you can Save to Files or AirDrop it. Mixes save as 320 kbps MP3. Inside claude.ai, which doesn't allow MP3 downloads, they save as WebM (Chrome, Edge) or MP4 audio (Safari).
Use your library
Open it with LIBRARY at the top of the page or LOAD on a deck. Link a rekordbox or Engine DJ USB, import a rekordbox XML export or link a music folder there. See .
Mix two tracks
Play deck 1, load deck 2, press SYNC on deck 2, line up the beats in the stacked waveform, then bring it in with the crossfader.
Knobs
Drag up or down, scroll, or use the arrow keys. Double-click returns a knob to its centre.
CONTROLLER
At the top of the page, switches the layout. Classic is the original two-deck layout. Mixstream Pro follows that unit's top panel: big platters, SYNC / CUE / PLAY down the left of each deck, the pitch fader with bend +/− on the right, one row of 4 pads (press the lit pad mode again for pads 5–8), and a centre screen with LOAD 1 / BROWSE / LOAD 2 and FX switches. Pick an effect in the middle, then click a channel's switch to latch it on, or hold the switch for a momentary effect.

DDJ-FLX4 follows that controller's top panel: IN / OUT / 4 BEAT/EXIT / CUE/LOOP CALL ◀ ▶ / BEAT SYNC across the top of each deck, SHIFT, CUE and PLAY down the left, TEMPO on the right, the level meters between the channel strips, MASTER LEVEL top right and a working BEAT FX section (channel 1, 2 or both, FX SELECT, BEAT ◀ ▶ for the echo time, LEVEL/DEPTH, ON/OFF). IN then OUT makes a loop on the beat; CUE/LOOP CALL halves or doubles a loop, or jumps between hot cues.

DDJ-FLX2 follows its smaller panel: no TRIM, master knob or meters (they keep their settings), and you change pad mode like on the unit: SHIFT, then BEAT SYNC, then one of the top four pads (HOT CUE, PAD FX, LOOP, SAMPLER). The labels over the pads work as a shortcut. On-screen SHIFT: click it, then press the next button.

Every layout runs the same decks, and this browser remembers your choice. The controller layouts need a wide screen; phones show Classic.

Decks and transport

PLAY
Starts and pauses the deck.
CUE
While playing: jumps back to the cue point and stops. Paused on the cue point: hold to preview, let go to return. Paused anywhere else: sets the cue point there. The amber triangle on the waveform marks it.
CUE → PLAY
Hold CUE to preview, slide onto PLAY (it lights up) and let go to keep the track playing.
Jog wheel
While playing, turn it to speed the track up (clockwise) or slow it down (anticlockwise) while you keep turning; the faster you turn, the bigger the push, up to 20%. The track never stops, and it glides back to normal speed when you stop turning. Watch the phase meter above the waveforms to see when the beats line up. While paused, drag it to scrub; one turn is 1.8 seconds, like a 33⅓ rpm record.
TEMPO
±16%. Down is faster, like a CDJ. Double-click resets to 0%. There is no key lock, so tempo also changes the pitch.
+ / −
Beside each tempo fader. Each press moves the tempo to the next exact 0.01 BPM, so you can land on a round tempo such as 128.00. Hold Shift for 0.1 BPM steps, or hold the button to keep stepping.
SYNC
Matches this deck's tempo to the other deck. It doesn't line up the beats; do that with the jog wheel.

Pads

Pick the pad mode with the buttons above the pads.

HOT CUE
Click an empty pad to set a hot cue at the playhead; click it again to jump there. Shift-click or right-click clears it. Cues are lettered A–H, each in its own colour: ABCDEFGH
BEAT LOOP
Loops 1/4 up to 32 beats, starting on the closest beat of the grid (any beat, 1 to 4, not just the downbeat) so loops always sit on the beat. If that beat is just ahead, the loop kicks in when the track reaches it. Press the lit pad again to leave the loop.
PAD FX
Hold a pad to play its effect; let go to stop. Shift-click latches an effect on until you Shift-click it again. Timing follows the deck's BPM. ECHO repeats every half beat and rings out after you let go. REVERB adds a room that also rings out. FLANGER sweeps a jet-like comb. TRANS chops the sound in 1/8 notes on the grid. SWEEP raises a high-pass filter over 4 beats. CRUSH turns the sound lo-fi. ROLL 1/4 and ROLL 1/8 repeat a tiny slice of the beat, then carry on where the track would have been, as if it had kept playing underneath.
GRID
Fixes the beat grid. ◀ BEAT and BEAT ▶ change which beat counts as the 1. ◀ 5ms and 5ms ▶ slide the whole grid. BAR 1 HERE puts a downbeat at the playhead. BPM −.01 and +.01 fine-tune the tempo. RESET goes back to the analysed grid.

Waveforms and grid

The panel at the top stacks deck 1 above deck 2 on the same time scale, so when both are synced their beats are the same distance apart and you only need to line them up.

Phase meter
Above the waveforms, beside the key match. With both decks playing at the same tempo, the dot shows how far deck 2's beats are from deck 1's, and the text gives the gap in milliseconds. Nudge with the jog wheel until it turns green and says Locked (within 8 ms).
Red line
The 1 of every bar.
White ticks
Beats 2, 3 and 4.
Coloured flag
A hot cue, with its letter.
Amber triangle
The cue point.
Bright and faded
Kick and bass are drawn bright over the faded full range, so beats show as pulses.
Drag
Grab a waveform to scrub. A playing deck pauses while you hold and carries on when you let go.
Overview
The strip under each track title shows the whole track. Click it to jump.

Mixer

TRIM
Input gain, ±12 dB.
HI / MID / LOW
EQ. Turn fully left to cut that band completely, up to +6 dB to the right.
FILTER
Left is a low-pass that removes the highs, right is a high-pass that removes the lows. Centre is off.
Channel fader
Each deck's volume, with its level meter beside it.
Crossfader
Blends between deck 1 on the left and deck 2 on the right. Double-click centres it.
MASTER
Overall level, with the meter under it.

Keys and harmonic mixing

Every track gets a Camelot code such as 8A. Numbers go round a wheel of 12; A is minor, B is major.

Mixes well
The same code, the same number with the other letter, or one step either way on the same letter (8A with 7A or 9A).
Camelot wheel
Deck 1's key is filled blue and deck 2's orange. Keys that mix well with a deck are tinted in its colour. WHEEL at the top of the page shows or hides it, and the waveforms widen to fill the space; this browser remembers your choice.
Key match line
Above the waveforms, says whether the two decks' keys mix well.
8A *
The tempo fader has moved the pitch a semitone or more, so this is the key you now hear. Hover for the original.
8A ?
Low confidence, usually because the track is mostly drums.

Library, rekordbox and Engine DJ

Nothing is uploaded: files are read in your browser and stay on your computer. The library isn't remembered, so link it again after reloading the page.

rekordbox USB
Plug in a USB exported from rekordbox and press LINK FOLDER OR USB, then pick the drive. Its playlists, beat grids, keys and hot cues come with it. If your browser won't let you pick the top of the drive, put the PIONEER and Contents folders in one folder and pick that.
rekordbox XML
In rekordbox choose File › Export Collection in xml format. Press IMPORT REKORDBOX XML and pick that file, then LINK FOLDER OR USB on the folder your music is in, for example Music.
Engine DJ
Press LINK FOLDER OR USB and pick an Engine DJ USB, or the folder that holds your Engine Library on this computer (usually Music). Its collection and playlists appear with Engine's BPM and key. Beat grids and cues are worked out here rather than taken from Engine. Close Engine DJ first so its latest changes are saved to the library.
Any folder
LINK FOLDER OR USB on its own lists every audio file in the folder. Tracks are analysed when you load them.
Opening it
LIBRARY at the top of the page opens it for either deck. LOAD on a deck opens it for that deck: its load button is lit on every row, and double-clicking a track loads it there. The library closes once a track starts loading; Escape or CLOSE shut it without loading.
Load
Press 1 or 2 on a row. Grey rows aren't linked to a file yet.
Orange glow
Tracks that mix well with the song the crowd is hearing: a compatible Camelot key and a tempo within 6% (half or double time counts too). If both decks are playing, it follows the louder one in the mix. The note above the list says which song it's matching.
✓ PLAYED
Tracks you've played from the library for at least 10 seconds this session. They're dimmed so you don't play them twice.
Sort and search
Click a column heading to sort, again to reverse. KEY sorts round the Camelot wheel. Search matches title, artist, key and BPM.
Plan a set
PLAN SET at the top of the page builds a running order. Add songs from your phone or computer, or use any library playlist, then answer three questions: what kind of set (Ramp up, Mountain, Waves, Steady groove or Wind down), how long, and whether key jumps of two steps are allowed. Songs without a BPM or key are analysed first. Each row says how it mixes in from the one before. Press 1 or 2 to load it, or SAVE AS PLAYLIST to keep the order in the library.
Auto DJ
Plan a set, then press ▶ AUTO DJ THIS SET (or AUTO DJ in the toolbar). It plays the set in order by itself. Near the end of each track it waits for an 8-bar phrase, syncs the next track's tempo, drops its bar 1 on the beat and crossfades over 16 bars, swapping the bass halfway so two kick drums never clash. Between mixes the playing track eases back to its own tempo. Press AUTO DJ again to stop: both decks keep playing and are yours. Press REC first to record the whole set.
Read-only
Changes made here, such as cues and grid edits, are never written back to rekordbox or Engine DJ.

DDJ-FLX4 controller

The FLX4 works in Chrome or Edge on a computer, using the desktop version (double-click Start JustGood Sets). The claude.ai link can't reach USB devices.

Connect
Close rekordbox and any other DJ app (on Windows only one app can use the controller), plug in the FLX4, press CONNECT at the top of the page and allow MIDI when Chrome asks. The button shows FLX4 ✓ once it's found; after that it reconnects by itself.
Sound
In Windows Sound settings, choose DDJ-FLX4 as the output to hear the music through the FLX4's master output.
Decks
PLAY, CUE (with the usual hold-to-preview), SYNC and SHIFT + CUE (back to the start) work as labelled. TEMPO, TRIM, EQ, CFX (the filter) and the channel faders and crossfader move the matching controls on screen.
Jog wheels
Turn the outer ring while playing to nudge the beats into line. Touch the top to hold the record: it stops, turning scrubs, and letting go plays on. While paused, any turn scrubs.
Pad modes
HOT CUE → HOT CUE, PAD FX 1 → PAD FX, BEAT JUMP → BEAT LOOP, SAMPLER → GRID. SHIFT + pad clears a hot cue, or latches a pad effect.
Loops
LOOP IN / 4 BEAT starts a 4-beat loop on the nearest beat; LOOP OUT or RELOOP/EXIT leaves it; CUE/LOOP CALL ◀ ▶ halve and double it.
Library
Turn BROWSE to open the library and move through the tracks; press LOAD 1 or LOAD 2 to load the highlighted one. Press BROWSE to close it.
Lights
PLAY, CUE, SYNC, the pad modes, the pads and the channel level meters follow the page.

Analysis and lights

BPM and grid
Found by searching the whole track for the steadiest tempo and beat position. The first strong beat is taken as bar 1; fix it with the GRID pads if it lands on the wrong beat.
Key
Detected with Essentia.js using a key profile tuned for electronic music, with a built-in detector as a fallback. The line at the bottom of the page says which one is running.
rekordbox data
Tracks from rekordbox keep their own grid, key and cues instead.
Light show
Each deck runs its own lights on its beat: a glow on its side of the room, brightest on the 1, and three beams from its corner that sweep across and back every bar, with the brightest flash chasing from beam to beam. The beam colours change every bar. The louder deck also fires a soft white flash on each 1, bigger at the start of every 16-beat phrase. Everything follows each channel's volume and the crossfader, so a faded-out deck goes dark. If your system is set to reduce motion, you get a steady glow with no movement or flashing.
