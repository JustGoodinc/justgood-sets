JustGood Sets - desktop version (for the Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 and DDJ-FLX2; the FLX2 needs no driver either)

1. Close rekordbox, Serato or any other DJ app (Windows lets only one app use the controller).
2. Plug the DDJ-FLX4 into this computer over USB.
3. Plug your speakers into the FLX4's MASTER OUT and headphones into its PHONES socket.
   Once connected, the app plays its sound through the FLX4, which the MASTER LEVEL knob needs:
   that knob works inside the FLX4 and only turns down sound that plays through it.
   If "SOUND -> FLX4" appears at the top, press it (Chrome asks for the microphone only to list
   sound devices; nothing is recorded), or set "DDJ-FLX4" as the output in Windows Sound settings.
4. Double-click "Start JustGood Sets.bat". Chrome opens the app; keep the black window open while you play.
5. Press CONNECT at the top of the page and click Allow when Chrome asks about MIDI devices.
   The button shows "FLX4 ✓" when the controller is found.

Help (top of the page) > "DDJ-FLX4 controller" explains what each control does.
To stop, close Chrome's tab and the black window.
